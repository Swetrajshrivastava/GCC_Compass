# Local evidence corpus

`government-india-gcc-report-2025.pdf` is the five-page Government of India / PIB report, “From Policy to Prosperity: GCCs Leading India’s Growth Journey,” published December 11, 2025.

- Publisher source: https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/dec/doc20251211727201.pdf
- Catalogue entry: `Government of India GCC report 2025` in `../sources.csv`
- The app extracts page text at runtime and displays short retrieved passages with page numbers and the publisher link.
- This is one document, not a comprehensive research corpus. Add further documents only with source, date, status, and page-level citation metadata.

The NASSCOM-Zinnov 2026 PDF is intentionally not bundled: its extracted text was not legible enough for reliable passage search in this prototype. Its source remains listed in the Evidence Desk; do not treat its catalogue entry as an extracted citation.
